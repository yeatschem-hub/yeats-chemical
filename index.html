About Yeats Chemical

Shanghai Yeats Chemical Co., Ltd. is a joint venture integrating R&D, production, and sales of lignin-based products. We have established a strong brand image in China's chemical industry with reliable product quality and well-developed market layout.

Using high-quality raw materials including coniferous pine, reed, and bamboo pulp, supported by mature production technology and strict quality control systems, we have formed a core product portfolio with stable performance and superior quality.

Our sales network covers all provinces and cities in China, and our products are exported to Japan, South Korea, Mexico, Australia, India, Turkey and many other countries, earning wide recognition and trust from global customers.
